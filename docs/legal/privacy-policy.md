# Privacy Policy — Valence

**Last updated: 21 June 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Valence** ("Valence", "we", "us") collects, uses,
and protects your information when you use the Valence mobile application (the "App").

Valence is a coaching-accountability tool that connects fitness coaches with their
clients for daily tracking. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

If you have any questions, contact us at **support@valence.app**.

---

## 1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yassine Dsouli (sole operator of Valence), based in Tunisia.
For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, we act as the **data controller**
for the personal data described below.

---

## 2. Information we collect

We only collect what the App needs to function. We do **not** sell your data, and we
do **not** use it for advertising.

### a) Information you provide
- **Account details:** email address, display name, and your role (coach or client).
- **Profile & fitness data (clients):** age, biological sex, height, current and goal
  weight, activity level, fitness goal, and the macro targets calculated from them.
- **Daily logs (clients):** meals and their nutrition values, photos of meals you
  choose to scan, water intake, sleep, body weight, completed workouts, and any
  custom habits your coach sets.
- **Coaching data (coaches):** workout templates, client assignments, macro targets,
  habit definitions, and notes you write about your clients.
- **Communications:** messages you send us at support@valence.app.

### b) Information collected automatically
- **Device & technical data** needed to run the service securely (e.g. app-integrity
  signals via Firebase App Check, and standard connection data processed by our
  infrastructure providers).
- **Notification preferences** for daily reminders. These reminders are scheduled
  **on your device** — the schedule itself is stored locally, not on our servers.

We do **not** currently use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- create and manage your account and authenticate you;
- provide core features (logging, tracking, workout assignment, adherence summaries);
- analyze meal photos and descriptions to estimate nutrition (see Section 5);
- send the daily reminders you enable;
- manage coach subscriptions and client limits;
- keep the service secure and prevent abuse;
- respond to your support requests and comply with legal obligations.

---

## 4. The coach–client relationship

Valence is built for coaches and clients to share accountability data.

- If you are a **client** linked to a coach, **your coach can see** the fitness and
  logging data described above (meals, photos you log, weight, sleep, habits, workout
  completion, and adherence summaries). This is the core purpose of the App.
- If you are a **coach**, the data your clients generate is visible to you for the
  purpose of coaching them. You agree to handle it responsibly and only for coaching.
- You can unlink from a coach at any time, and you can delete your account (Section 8).

Valence does **not** include in-app chat. Personal messaging happens outside the App.

---

## 5. AI meal analysis (Google Gemini via Firebase)

When you scan or describe a meal, the photo and/or text is sent to **Google's Gemini
model through Firebase AI Logic** to estimate the food and its nutrition. The request
is proxied by Firebase; we do not embed any AI key in the App.

- Google processes this content to return the estimate. Google's handling is governed
  by the Firebase and Google Cloud terms.
- Nutrition estimates are **approximate** and provided for convenience only — they are
  not medical or dietary advice (see our Terms of Service).

---

## 6. Service providers (processors)

We rely on **Google Firebase** (a Google service) for our backend:
- **Firebase Authentication** — sign-in and account management;
- **Cloud Firestore** — storing your account, logs, and coaching data;
- **Firebase Storage** — storing images you upload;
- **Firebase AI Logic** — the AI meal analysis described above;
- **Firebase App Check** — protecting the backend from abuse.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.
See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

---

## 7. Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your
account, we delete your associated data as described in Section 8. We may retain limited
information where required by law or to resolve disputes.

---

## 8. Your rights and choices

You can, at any time:
- **Access and update** your profile data in the App;
- **Delete your account** from **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently removes
  your account and your associated data (your logs, assigned workouts, and profile).
  For coaches, your clients are unlinked and keep their own accounts;
- **Manage notifications** in the App or your device settings;
- **Contact us** at support@valence.app to exercise data-protection rights (access,
  correction, deletion, objection, portability) where applicable law provides them,
  including the GDPR for users in the European Economic Area.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

---

## 9. Data security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures, including authenticated access,
per-document access rules in Firestore, and app-integrity verification via App Check.
No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

---

## 10. Children's privacy

Valence is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from
children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will
delete it.

---

## 11. International transfers

Your data is processed on Google's infrastructure, which may store and process data in
data centers outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for
such transfers.

---

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date and,
for material changes, notify you in the App. Continued use after changes means you accept
the updated Policy.
