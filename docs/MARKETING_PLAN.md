## 1. Marketing Strategy Overview

### The Core Thesis

Valence's marketing works because of one fundamental truth: **coaches are the marketing engine.**

Every coach who joins and uses Valence daily will be seen by:
- 10–30 clients (who use the client app, tell their friends)
- Hundreds of Instagram/TikTok followers (coaches share their business tools)
- Peers in their coaching community (Facebook Groups, WhatsApp groups, Discord servers)

The job of Valence marketing is **not** to convince millions of coaches one by one. It is to:
1. Get the right 50 coaches to fall in love with it during beta
2. Turn those 50 coaches into vocal advocates
3. Let word-of-mouth + content marketing compound from there

### Growth Model: Product-Led Growth (PLG) + Community

```
Coach discovers Valence (organic/content/ads)
         ↓
Signs up free (zero friction, no credit card)
         ↓
Invites clients (5 free slots) → clients join
         ↓
Hits 5-client limit OR sees the Pro features
         ↓
Converts to Pro ($49/mo)
         ↓
Loves the product → posts about it → new coaches find Valence
         ↓
              [Loop repeats]
```

### Marketing Budget Philosophy

In Year 1, **70% of the marketing budget goes to content + community** (low CAC, compounding returns), and only 30% to paid ads (immediate but expensive). This flips in Year 2 as we have case studies and social proof to make paid ads more efficient.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP)

### ICP #1 — "The Ambitious Solo Coach" (Primary Target, 60% of focus)

**Profile:**
- Age: 25–38
- Income: $2k–$6k/month from coaching
- Clients: 8–25 online clients
- Platforms currently used: WhatsApp, Google Sheets, Instagram DMs, PDF plans
- Tech-savvy: Uses apps daily, comfortable with new tools
- Pain: Spending 2–3 hours/day on admin instead of coaching
- Goal: Get to 30+ clients without burning out

**Where they live online:**
- Instagram (posts about coaching 3–5x/week)
- TikTok (short coaching tips content)
- Facebook groups: "Online Fitness Coaches", "PT Business Growth", "Fitness Business Owners"
- Reddit: r/personaltraining, r/onlinefitness
- YouTube: Watches "how to grow your coaching business" content

**Trigger to buy:**
- Hits the free tier 5-client limit
- Sees another coach using Valence on Instagram
- Frustrated by a specific tool limitation (WhatsApp chaos, Google Sheets breaking)
- Gets a referral from a coach they respect

**Message that resonates:**
> "Stop running your business like it's 2015. See exactly who's on track and who's about to quit — before they quit."

---

### ICP #2 — "The Established Coach Ready to Scale" (Secondary Target, 25% of focus)

**Profile:**
- Age: 30–45
- Income: $6k–$20k/month from coaching
- Clients: 25–60
- Currently using: Trainerize or TrueCoach (frustrated with them)
- Pain: Existing tool feels corporate, support is slow, features haven't improved in 2 years
- Goal: Find a platform they can grow with that won't be acquired and enshittified

**Trigger to buy:**
- TrueCoach/Trainerize price increase notification
- Feature request ignored for the 5th time
- Sees Valence's AI food analysis feature demo

**Message that resonates:**
> "Built by coaches, not by a private equity firm. Finally, a platform that actually evolves."

---

### ICP #3 — "The Gym Owner / Multi-Coach Business" (Elite Target, 15% of focus)

**Profile:**
- Age: 30–55
- Revenue: $50k–$500k/year from coaching memberships
- Team: 2–10 coaches
- Pain: No unified dashboard across coaches; clients get inconsistent experience
- Goal: Standardize coaching quality, reduce admin overhead, look more professional

**Trigger to buy:**
- A staff coach already using Valence and advocating internally
- Competitive pressure from a nearby gym offering better client tech
- Attending a fitness business conference and seeing a demo

**Message that resonates:**
> "Give every coach on your team a professional client dashboard. One platform. One brand. Full visibility."

## 3. Positioning & Messaging Framework

### Core Positioning Statement

**For** fitness coaches who are drowning in fragmented tools,  
**Valence** is the all-in-one coaching platform  
**that** gives coaches real-time visibility into every client and gives clients a beautiful daily experience —  
**unlike** Trainerize and TrueCoach, which have become bloated, corporate, and stagnant,  
**Valence** is modern, AI-powered, and obsessively designed for how coaches actually work.

**Level 1 — Core (always true):** "The Power of Connection"  
**Level 2 — Functional:** "See who's on track. Stop who's about to quit."  
**Level 3 — Emotional:** "Run a coaching business you're actually proud of."  
**Level 4 — Social:** "Your clients will brag about your app."

|----------|-------------|-------------|----------------|

| Gym owner | Inconsistent coach quality | "Give your whole team one professional platform" | Book a demo |
| Coach's clients (indirect) | Disconnected, unmotivated | "Your coach cares enough to use the best tools for you" | Download your invite |

| Objection | Response |

## 4. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Beta Launch (Months 1–2) — Prove It Works

**Goal:** 50 active coaches, 10+ giving written testimonials, 3 case studies

**Tactics:**
1. **Personal outreach** to 200 coaches in Instagram DMs / LinkedIn: "Hey [Name], I've been following your content — you share a lot about the admin side of coaching. Built something I think you'd genuinely love. Can I show you for 10 minutes?"
2. **Facebook Group seeding** in "Online Fitness Business" groups: share helpful tips, mention Valence naturally (not spammy), join conversations about tools
3. **Reddit posts** in r/personaltraining: "I built a free app for online coaches — would love beta feedback" (honest, transparent)
4. **Fitness educator partnerships:** Email 20 online personal training course creators (NASM, ISSA, etc.) and ask to offer Valence free to their students
5. **First coach case study** at Day 30: one coach's story with before/after admin time

**Success metrics:**
- 50 coaches using the app weekly
- 3 coaches publicly posting about Valence
- Net Promoter Score (first survey): > 40

---

### Phase 2: PMF + First Revenue (Months 3–6) — Find the Message That Converts

**Goal:** $5k MRR, understand which message/channel has lowest CAC

**Tactics:**
1. Launch **blog** (2 posts/week) targeting SEO keywords coaches search
2. First **YouTube tutorial series** (5 videos: "How I manage 30 clients in under 1 hour/day")
3. **Case study content** on LinkedIn + Instagram (real coaches, real numbers)
4. **Referral program** soft-launch: "Give a coach 1 month free, get 1 month free"
5. **Facebook Ads** A/B test: 3 ad variants, $500/month test budget, identify winner
6. **Coach community call** monthly (Zoom): free education + soft Valence pitch at end

**Success metrics:**
- $5k MRR by Month 6
- Blog: 500 organic visitors/month
- Identify 1 channel with CAC < $75

---

### Phase 3: Scale What Works (Months 7–12) — Double Down

**Goal:** $30k MRR, 300+ Pro coaches, first Elite clients

**Tactics:**
1. **Scale winning ad channel** (2× budget on what worked in Phase 2)
2. **Affiliate program** official launch: coaches earn 20% recurring commission for 12 months
3. **YouTube channel** growth: 2 videos/week, aim for 5,000 subscribers
4. **Product Hunt launch** (Month 8): coordinated upvote campaign
5. **PR push** (Month 9): TechCrunch, Fitness Business Insider, Morning Brew
6. **Elite tier launch** (Month 7): direct outreach to gyms via LinkedIn and fitness conferences
7. **First App Store feature push** (Month 10): submit to Apple editorial, target "Apps We Love"

**Success metrics:**
- $30k MRR by Month 12
- 3,000+ total coaches registered
- YouTube: 2,000 subscribers
- App Store rating: 4.7+

### Channel Deep-Dives

#### SEO — The Compound Engine
SEO is the single most important long-term channel. Fitness coaches search Google constantly for:
- "best app for online fitness coaches"
- "how to track clients online coaching"
- "online personal training software"
- "client tracking app fitness coach"
- "replace trainerize alternative"

A well-executed SEO strategy can bring 500–2,000 coach signups/month by Month 18 with near-zero ongoing CAC. See full SEO strategy in Section 6.

#### YouTube — Authority + Discovery
YouTube is where coaches go to learn. A "Valence Coach Tips" channel teaching coaches *how to run a better business* (with Valence as the natural tool) is a compounding asset. One video ranking #1 for "how to manage online coaching clients" can drive 50–100 coach signups per month indefinitely.

#### Instagram — Brand Proof
Instagram isn't a primary acquisition channel — it's a **social proof** channel. When a prospect googles Valence, they check Instagram. A beautiful, active Instagram profile with real coach testimonials converts skeptics. Coaches also use Instagram to validate "is this legit?" before upgrading.

#### Facebook Groups — Where Coaches Already Are
There are active Facebook Groups with 10,000–50,000 members of fitness coaches. Participating helpfully (not spammily) in these communities drives highly qualified signups. Target groups:
- Online Fitness Business (47k members)
- PT Business Growth (32k members)
- Fitness Business Mentorship Community (28k members)
- NASM Certified Trainers (25k members)

**What works for this audience:**
- Screen recordings of the actual app (coaches want to see what they're getting)

- Complex copy with too many features listed
- Ads that look too "corporate" (coaches are DIY entrepreneurs, they distrust polished corporate marketing)

## 11. Referral & Affiliate Program

### Referral Program ("Give a Month, Get a Month")

**Mechanics:**
- Every Pro coach gets a unique referral link in their dashboard
- When a coach they refer upgrades to Pro: referrer gets 1 free month; referred coach gets 1 free month
- No cap on referrals — a top referrer could earn months of free Pro indefinitely

**Why this works:**
- Coaches talk to coaches constantly (community is tight)
- Zero cash outlay — rewards are product months (90% gross margin)
- The referred coach is highly pre-qualified (their friend already sold them on it)

**Target: 25% of Pro upgrades from referral by Month 12**

---

### Affiliate Program ("Earn 20% Forever")

Designed for coaches with audiences (YouTubers, course creators, newsletter writers, podcast hosts).

**Structure:**
- **Commission:** 20% of monthly subscription, recurring for 12 months
- **Cookie window:** 90 days
- **Minimum payout:** $50 (paid monthly via PayPal/Stripe)
- **Affiliate dashboard:** Real-time clicks, signups, conversions, earnings

**At $49/month Pro:** Affiliate earns $9.80/month per referred coach for 12 months = $117.60 per converted referral  
**At $199/month Elite:** Affiliate earns $39.80/month for 12 months = $477.60 per converted referral

**Positioning to affiliates:**
