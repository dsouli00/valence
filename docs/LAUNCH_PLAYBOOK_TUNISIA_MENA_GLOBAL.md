# VALENCE — Launch Playbook (Tunisia → MENA → Global)

This is a practical execution plan for your rollout and company decision path.

---

## 1) Direct Answer: Open a Startup Now or Not?

Short answer: **Start with a lightweight legal setup fast, not “no company.”**

Recommended path:
1. **Immediately launch and sell** to validate demand.
2. **Create a legal entity early** (before meaningful recurring revenue, contracts, or hiring).
3. Use a staged structure:
   - Stage A: simple Tunisian setup to operate and invoice locally
   - Stage B: upgrade structure when expanding to MENA/global (payments, tax, IP, fundraising)

Why:
- You reduce personal legal/tax risk.
- You can sign B2B deals and payment contracts properly.
- You keep flexibility while avoiding heavy overhead too early.

Do **not** stay indefinitely as “just deploy and collect money personally.”

---

## 2) 3-Stage Expansion Strategy

## Stage 1 — Tunisia (0–6 months)

### Primary goal
Get real paying coach customers and proven retention.

### Focus KPIs
- 20–50 active coaches
- >=30% month-2 coach retention
- clear onboarding-to-paid conversion signal

### Execution
1. Pick one ICP first (solo online coaches).
2. Close first 10 paying coaches manually (founder-led sales).
3. Weekly product iteration from direct user calls.
4. Build local trust assets:
   - Arabic/French/English landing pages
   - local success stories
   - clear support channel (WhatsApp + email)

### Legal/ops minimum
- Entity for invoicing/compliance
- Business bank account
- accounting/tax workflow
- terms/privacy policy that match current app behavior

---

## Stage 2 — MENA (6–18 months)

### Primary goal
Replicate Tunisia playbook in 2–3 MENA markets.

### Market selection order (suggested)
1. Morocco (language/cultural adjacency)
2. GCC test market (higher ARPU, stronger willingness to pay)
3. Egypt or KSA/UAE based on traction channel performance

### Execution
1. Localized onboarding and support.
2. Country-specific pricing tests (do not force one global price too early).
3. Regional partnerships:
   - coach communities
   - fitness educators
   - gym micro-networks
4. Add market-by-market legal/tax checks before scale campaigns.

### KPI gates before moving to next market
- >=15 paying coaches per new country
- stable activation and retention
- repeatable acquisition channel with healthy CAC

---

## Stage 3 — International (18+ months)

### Primary goal
Scale through repeatable channels, not founder-only selling.

### Execution
1. Define one global core SKU + regional pricing adjustments.
2. Build partner/referral engine.
3. Add structured sales + customer success workflows.
4. Prepare investment/fundraising package only after strong unit signals.

### Scale prerequisites
- clear payback period
- strong retention curve
- reliable legal/tax/payment infrastructure for cross-border operations

## 4) Decision Framework (Keep You Safe)

Choose **“incorporate now”** when any of these is true:
- You are collecting recurring payments.
- You are signing contracts (B2B/gym/partner).
- You are hiring or paying contractors regularly.
- You need stronger liability/IP protection.

Choose **“delay full complex structure”** only if:
- you are pre-revenue or near-zero revenue,
- no contracts,
- short validation window.

Practical middle ground:
- start with a lean legal setup now,
- avoid expensive complex structuring until traction confirms.

---

## 5) Non-Negotiables for This Project

1. Keep product claims 100% aligned with real implemented features.
2. Prioritize retention over vanity growth.
3. Never expand markets without legal/tax/payment readiness.
4. Build for cashflow discipline first, fundraising optional later.
